## Backlog refinement

Prepared by Codex backlog refinement. This describes planned work; implementation has not started.

### Intended outcome

Approve work from the board or table without opening each ticket. Make scope approval and acceptance of completed work separate, clearly named actions.

### Scope and approach

Provide Approve scope for unapproved planned work, and Accept into Done for tickets in Review. Show the result inline. Batch actions share the selection model from #17. Scope approval alone should not silently select, start, or complete a ticket.

### Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A human can approve a ticket’s scope from its card/row; approved and inherited-scope states are visually distinguishable.
- [ ] A human can accept a Review ticket into Done directly; its summary and evidence remain accessible before accepting.
- [ ] Selected tickets can receive the same eligible approval action with a visible selection count; ineligible items are identified rather than silently changed.
- [ ] Agent requests cannot approve scope or accept work into Done, including through the new bulk path.
- [ ] Stale revisions and partial failures are shown per ticket; successful changes retain human attribution and do not overwrite unrelated fields.

### Implementation notes and related work

Start with web/TicketCard.tsx, web/TableView.tsx, web/ProjectPage.tsx and src/store.ts authority/history. Related: #17. Keep the two meanings of approval explicit in copy and action availability.

### Human review / verification

Approve scope on a single item and selected items, then accept a reviewed item. Check that status is unchanged by scope approval, agent calls remain rejected, and a stale item produces a clear result.
