- Serialize the change with current ticket revision and assignment/run identity checks.
- Preserve prior assignment, run, worktree, logs and attribution in history; release only the obsolete claim.
- Allow the new actor to claim, report progress and submit human review using its own identity.
- Do not grant scope approval, reset attempt limits, self-accept an implementation, or forge human attribution.
- Cover active-process refusal, stale revisions, unauthorized workers, successful stopped-run takeover, and unrelated assignment preservation.
