## Backlog refinement

Prepared by Codex backlog refinement. This describes planned work; implementation has not started.

### Intended outcome

Connect overlapping tickets and consolidate genuine duplicates without losing their discussion, images, decisions, or history.

### Scope and approach

Support a lightweight Related tickets relationship and a separate explicit merge action that chooses a surviving ticket. Show a reviewable merge preview. Retain the source ticket as an archived duplicate pointing to the survivor rather than deleting its record; preserve original attribution and links.

### Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Find related tickets by number/title, add or remove a relationship, and see reciprocal links in both details and agent context without treating related work as a blocking dependency.
- [ ] A merge preview identifies the survivor and source, content to preserve, and conflicts in parent, status, owner, priority, or acceptance criteria; conflicting metadata is not silently overwritten.
- [ ] The survivor retains access to source descriptions, comments, attachments/annotations, and decision/rule references with their original provenance. Existing links to the source still resolve and show the survivor.
- [ ] Source tickets are explicitly marked as duplicates and excluded from ordinary active views after merge; this does not claim the work was completed or accepted into Done.
- [ ] Incoming parent/dependency links are handled deliberately, with cycle/self-link validation, and both records retain an audit trail.
- [ ] Merge uses revision checks on every affected record and reports partial failure/recovery clearly; repeated requests must not duplicate content. Related links remain independently editable.

### Implementation notes and related work

Start with src/types.ts, src/store.ts relationship/context/archive behavior, and web/RecordDetail.tsx. Add CLI/MCP support so agents can identify duplicates and retrieve related context. Coordinate with #23; related, duplicate, parent, and dependency are distinct relationships.

### Human review / verification

Relate two tickets, then preview and merge duplicates containing different comments/images and conflicting metadata. Follow old links, retrieve agent context, and simulate stale input. Confirm that no content or attribution disappears and no ticket is falsely accepted as Done.
