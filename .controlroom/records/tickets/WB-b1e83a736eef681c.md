I would like a way to click a swimlane and turn on/off the tickets in that swimline from being visible. For example backlog can be large and I want to turn off backlog and just view the other swimlanes. Maybe a little human eye icon that is in the swimlane header you can click and quickly turn on/off visibility.

## Backlog refinement

Prepared by Codex backlog refinement. This describes planned work; implementation has not started. Proposed behavior below remains subject to human scope review.

### Intended outcome

Temporarily hide tickets in a busy status column such as Backlog with one click, then restore them just as easily.

### Scope and approach

The Backlog example refers to workflow/status columns (called swimlanes in the request). Add an eye-style visibility toggle to each column header. Hide that column's ticket content across all parent/label groups, retaining a compact labeled header or equivalent visible restore control. This is a visibility preference, not an archive or ticket-status change.

Proposed default: remember hidden column IDs per project and saved view in personal UI preferences, without rewriting the shared view's search query. Keep visibility independent of existing horizontal group collapse. Provide Show all columns and a clear indication of hidden columns.

### Acceptance criteria

- [ ] One click hides Backlog's cards across all groups and leaves other columns usable. A visible control restores Backlog with one click.
- [ ] Header controls expose an accessible name and pressed/expanded state, work by keyboard, and clearly distinguish hidden from empty columns.
- [ ] No ticket, claim, stage, archive flag or parent relationship changes when toggling visibility.
- [ ] Search/filter changes compose with visibility without losing the user's query. Hidden columns remain identifiable, and Show all recovers from every column being hidden.
- [ ] Visibility is remembered for that project/view after refresh without leaking to another project/view; custom column rename/reorder preserves it by stable ID, and deleted IDs are safely ignored.
- [ ] Board keyboard navigation skips hidden cards, focus stays recoverable after hiding a focused column, and hidden content cannot receive invisible drag/drop or quick-create interactions.
- [ ] Counts and any bulk-action preview make their scope clear; hidden tickets are not silently selected for operations on visible tickets.
- [ ] Table/list views retain their existing behavior; switching back to the board restores its column visibility preferences.

### Implementation notes and related work

Inspect web/BoardView.tsx column rendering and keyboard navigation, web/ProjectPage.tsx view preferences and web/model.ts filters. Related: #44 quick archive, #23 ticket clutter, #18 status customization. Do not conflate this with existing parent-group collapse or automatically create a new shared saved view.

### Human review / verification

Hide Backlog on a populated parent-grouped board, filter the remaining tickets, restore Backlog and refresh. Switch views/projects and rename a custom column. Check keyboard recovery and the all-hidden state.
