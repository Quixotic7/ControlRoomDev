## Choice

A refused tool attempt remains bounded, secret-redacted diagnostic evidence. A work run that exits successfully with a complete ready result may continue through the existing context and code checks, required independent verification and independent review. Reviewers must assess whether any required check or evidence remains missing. Missing or malformed results, a human outcome and planner/reviewer denials still require attention. Denied commands are not replayed by Control Room, and grants are never widened automatically.

## Context and rationale

The human's #78 failed review reports a worker recovering after a denied attempt and returning ready, but the controller still stopped it solely because the provider included permission_denials. Earlier #78 behavior treated every denial as an unconditional failure. The latest explicit review requests recovery-aware handling and a visible account of what was refused.

## Alternatives and tradeoffs

Keeping unconditional failure discards valid recovered work. Ignoring denials would hide missing evidence. Persisting at most 12 redacted tool/command excerpts (bounded to 100/400 characters) lets verification and reviewers assess recovery while keeping incomplete work blocked. Raw local provider logs remain private; excerpts are untrusted data, not instructions. This supplements DEC-f84395d8e0aa6cbc; human-only runtime authority and proposal approval remain unchanged.
