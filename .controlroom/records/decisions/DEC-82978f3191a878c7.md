## Choice

Microtasks are ordinary checklist lines under a ticket's dedicated level-two Microtasks heading. Only those checklist lines are edited by the checklist controls. Questionnaires extend question comments with stable question IDs and validated metadata, plus readable prompts and submitted answers in their Markdown body.

## Context and rationale

Both humans and agents must read project knowledge without the application. Reusing ticket revisions for checklist edits and comment revisions for questionnaire edits prevents coordinated clients from silently overwriting each other. Browser-only answer drafts are separate from explicitly submitted, human-attributed answers.

## Alternatives and tradeoffs

Separate ticket IDs for every checkbox would clutter the board. A separate questionnaire database would duplicate durable project knowledge. Comment-based forms keep existing Needs-you and context behavior, but structured form metadata is more verbose than plain questions. Replacements and amendments preserve previous wording, answers and history. Drafts are local to a browser and do not expire; they are not automatically shared across browsers.
