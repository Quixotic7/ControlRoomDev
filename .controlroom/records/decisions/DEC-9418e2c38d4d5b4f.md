## Choice

Animate a progress-role ticket only when the local orchestrator verifies a running work process using its active run, PID and process start identity. Poll a minimal activity endpoint once per workspace every five seconds; clear the signal on failed requests and when returning to a suspended tab. Claims, timestamps, reports, workflow roles and percent estimates do not prove execution.

## Context and rationale

The human review of #28 and approved #72 explicitly rejected constant stage animation. Failed Review shares the progress role and must display its actual configured name. Preserve the larger waveform for verified work and respect reduced motion. Preserve attributed reports, stale/expired notices and current-session wall-clock timing, but label elapsed time as time in the stage, never active agent time.

## Alternatives and tradeoffs

Animating on stage, claims or recent reports is misleading when an agent stops. Managed process verification is available; external chat agents remain unverified rather than inferred active. A running process may be waiting internally, so the indicator promises process liveness, not useful output or completion. Polling has up to one interval of latency; requests time out after four seconds and clear the signal. No automatic status movement occurs.
